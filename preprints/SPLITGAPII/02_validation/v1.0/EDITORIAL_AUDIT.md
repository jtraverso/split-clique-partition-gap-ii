# Editorial review of preprint 1.0

The comparison baseline is draft 0.2 at commit 68d353c44d3eb1ac865b529f33804117fb6ed8c3. All eleven numbered equations agree with that draft. The English and Spanish texts have the same ten numbered results, mathematical expressions and bibliography.

The abstract states the zero-gap criterion per core vertex and indicates both recovery methods. Section 1 derives the residual score from the saving of a core clique and the opportunity cost of the pairs it uses. Lemma 4 gives the two intervals whose difference bounds the gap. Proposition 6 identifies the score and the feasible packing family when the residual changes. In the twelve-vertex example each deletion changes the residual used for the exact evaluation. Complementary slackness rules out integral score six in the middle row. The bound on the optimal coefficient applies to crossing-edge deletions starting from a complete assignment. Its exact value remains open.

Section 4 uses the endpoint-excess estimate. Section 5 distinguishes the leading asymptotic constant from the list-coloring threshold. Section 6 states the degree cap before applying it and accounts separately for each pair's contribution. The formalization companions identify the finite Lean results and the remaining arguments proved in the manuscript.

The four PDFs were rendered and inspected page by page (23 pages), including the figure, proof displays, bibliographies and printed URLs. Each manuscript prints the eight reference URLs. The edition carries the author's acknowledgments and AI disclosure. The package follows the manuscript, validation, reproducibility, integrity and formalization layout of Part I.
